TV Hub

TV Hub is a small private home-screen app for a family Android TV. It shows photos, videos and a set of reference documents on the television.

To keep the documents on the TV up to date, the app signs in once to the owner's own Google account with read-only access to Google Drive, reads one chosen folder of documents, and stores a copy on the television itself.

The app is used only by its owner and the owner's family. It is not offered to the public.
